# 周南先生醫談（九州大學醫學圖書館 シ-116） / Shūnan sensei idan [Medical Conversations of Master Shūnan]: Transcription Dataset

《周南先生醫談》是九州大學醫學圖書館和漢古醫書 シ-116 所藏一冊漢文寫本，館方書誌歸山縣周南。條列診病觀察、進食照護與治法取捨，並有朱墨眉批及欄旁小字。可用於研究近世日本漢文醫談的醫學判斷與讀寫痕跡。

This one-volume Literary Chinese manuscript of Shūnan sensei idan, attributed in the catalogue to Yamagata Shūnan, collects medical observations, comments on care and diet, and discussions of therapeutic choices. It preserves red marginal writing and short annotations.

底本共14幅、25面。其中18面有正文，共152正文欄，另錄內題、題簽、可辨副文本以及封底墨記。

The 14 scans identify 25 surfaces, including 18 written body faces with 152 main-text columns, plus titles, visible annotations and end matter.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 22 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。格內題頭與正文、朱墨眉批、欄旁小字及版心分層。卷首小字及分則數見疑讀。紙損、朱墨筆記與候選可回查同幅。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 22 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Body, headings, marginal writing and smaller annotations are identified separately. Damage, doubtful readings and uncertainty in the internal enumeration are recorded.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
